ELECTRONIC MUSIC PRODUCER · DJ · SWEDEN

SUNNY

SUNNY is a Sweden-based electronic music producer and DJ specializing in Tech House and Progressive House. His sound combines driving club grooves, melodic atmosphere and modern electronic production, spanning original music, remixes and high-energy DJ sets.

Built around the dancefloor but shaped by melody, SUNNY's work reimagines familiar songs through contemporary house production while developing an original sound of his own.
